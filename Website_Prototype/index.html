The TrabaHacks pipeline

Learn → Solve → Prove → Connect

A challenge-based experience that helps students enter industry with evidence, confidence, and a network they can carry forward.

01

Train

Students learn the foundations of supply chain and business operations.

02

Challenge

A partner shares a real problem, context, data, and success criteria.

03

Build

Teams of 3–4 create a practical prototype in a focused 36-hour competition sprint.

04

Present

Students show their reasoning, teamwork, business impact, and feasibility.

05

Connect

Scored work opens a door to sponsors and Hispanic companies hiring talent.

Our community

Hispanic students, universities, and companies.

TrabaHacks is a bridge between students, universities, and companies that need talent. Students get training and a chance to prove themselves. Universities get a way to connect their students with opportunity. Companies get a pipeline of pre-vetted talent.

For companies

Turn recruiting into proof.

Instead of judging talent only from resumes, companies can watch students solve a real problem under a deadline, then revisit that work when hiring.

For anchor partners

  • Bring a real supply-chain problem
  • Provide data, context, or requirements
  • Set judging criteria with your team
  • Review solutions with future talent
  • Identify high-potential candidates

For students and employers

  • Build a record of completed work
  • Show up with a multidisciplinary team
  • Practice modern business tools
  • Submit a solution you can stand behind
  • Connect Hispanic talent with opportunity
